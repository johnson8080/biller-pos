What it is

Biller POS is a point-of-sale system built for the Kenyan shop floor: one-handed operation on a phone, fast number entry at the till, and everything stored on the device by default — no internet needed to sell, restock or ring up.

Core promise: sell, stock, and know what you owe — even with a poor signal. When the network returns, the app syncs automatically.

Key benefits

  • Works offline by default — data is saved locally; an "Offline" banner appears during a connection loss.
  • Instant and fast — no server round-trips to ring a sale; receipts are instantly searchable by number.
  • Money for every payment method — cash, M-Pesa (STK Push to the customer), card, and Customer Debt on one checkout pad.
  • Business intelligence built in — weekly sales chart, top sellers, staff scoreboard, payment mix, wastage, and a monthly target with real-time progress.
  • Made for handhelds — light/dark themes (Forest, Ocean, Clay, Plum, Black) and 3 text sizes.
  • Trust built in — a 4-digit PIN to lock all edits and deletes, an activity history log, and nothing is visible until confirmed.

Getting started (5 steps)

  1. Install. Web: long-press the URL → Add to Home Screen. Android: install the APK and open it from your home screen.
  2. Open the guided tour. A friendly tour walks new shopkeepers through Sell, Cart, Orders, Debt, Suppliers and Reports. New shops auto-start it after first sale; manual: Settings → TAKE THE TOUR. Skippable anytime.
  3. Add a few products (Stock screen). Name, category, price, stock, supplier. Choose categories like Groceries/Beauty/Agrovet — presets exist; add your own.
  4. Set your monthly target. Dashboard tracks actual vs. target monthly sales (target card on the Reports screen).
  5. Go sell (Sell screen). Tap products or tap numbers, add discount, and checkout with cash, M-Pesa, card, or add to customer debt.
  6. Review (Reports screen). Sales per day, profit, best sellers, payment mix — all automatic after a sale.

Features

Sell & checkout

  • Fast number entry — tap to add products or type quantities; edit price, tax and discount live on the cart.
  • One-handed friendly — large, tapable targets; no jump between screens.
  • Discount & split payment — live discounts; mixed payments with per-tender amounts.
  • Receipts — share to WhatsApp, email, or print; PDF, DOCX or screenshot export, no server needed.
  • M-Pesa STK Push — Safaricom dials the customer's phone; you confirm with the PIN prompt on their phone. Record your Paybill/Till number in Settings → M-Pesa.
  • Card terminal — for card terminal providers.
  • Customer Debt — full debt ledger: add to debt, view and chase, due dates, reminders.

Stock & inventory

  • Products — add name, SKU, category, supplier, price, stock, photo. 30+ Kenya-specific categories; add your own in More → Categories.
  • Variants — sizes/colours with each variant having its own stock + price.
  • Weigh mode — for use by scale/spreadsheet based sales floorplan.
  • Shelf counting audit — count shelf stock, mark each item as MATCH / VARIANCE / OVER / UNDER, print. Finds over-supply before you walk the shelves.
  • Wastage & write-offs — full history, reason tags, costs, and tracking.
  • Delivery, receipting — stock enters when goods are bought.
  • Best-before warnings — 30/90/180-day markers, auto-reallocation.
  • Sales checker — see remaining stock after each sale; guardrails if you're running out.

Debt management

  • Add to Debt — record credit sales with due date and pin.
  • Chase & collect — track payments, print statements, view collections.
  • Debt card on the dock — shows total debt and due today at a glance.

Reports (dashboard)

  • Daily sales chart — updated automatically after each sale. Week view. Daily sales goal for each day.
  • Sales, profit, best sellers — see what sells and makes money, not just what moves most units.
  • Top sellers — the shop's best-selling items of the month with sales records.
  • Payment mix — cash vs. M-Pesa vs. card vs. debt split.
  • Staff scoreboard — who sold what, by person and by amount.
  • Wastage reports — track reasons and costs with each entry.
  • Monthly target — set a sales goal and track actual vs. target on the Reports screen, auto-updated.
  • Export PDF, DOCX, or screenshot — share the report with your team, accountant or client.

Security & trust

  • Shop PIN — a 4-digit PIN is required to edit stock, edit or delete orders, products, suppliers or shop data, and every write-off and restore.
  • History log — every add, edit, delete, write-off and restore on the till, with a purge option.
  • Payment mode lock — allow cash-only, M-Pesa-only, or card-only mode at checkout.
  • No-debt toggle — "No debt allowed" is on by default; off lets customers pay later via Debt.
  • Subscription — trial status per shop, monthly recurring plans (switch in Settings).
  • Export/Import — Settings → Export data (products, orders and settings as JSON) and Restore from backup.
